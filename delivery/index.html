Shipping and delivery policy

Effective 31 October 2026.

  2. When you get access

1. Digital service only

Cafe Insights is online software. Nothing physical is shipped, and there are no shipping or delivery charges.

2. When you get access

  • Free features: available instantly at dipayan8k.github.io/cafe-insights/app.
  • Paid plans (once launched): activated immediately after successful payment. A payment receipt and GST invoice, where applicable, are emailed to the address you give at checkout within 24 hours.
  • One-time reports (once launched): shown on screen immediately, with a downloadable copy.

3. If something doesn't arrive

If you paid and can't access what you bought, or didn't get a receipt within 24 hours, email business.dipayan.sayak@gmail.com with your transaction ID. We'll respond within 48 hours. If we can't give you access, you'll be refunded as described in our Cancellation and refund policy.
