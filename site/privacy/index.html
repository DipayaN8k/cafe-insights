3. Your sales and menu files

When you open a file in the app, it is read and analysed by software running inside your own web browser, using a technology called WebAssembly. The file, its contents and the results:

  • are not uploaded to us or to any other server;
  • are not stored by us; they stay in your browser's memory and are cleared when you close or reload the tab;
  • cannot be seen by us, our staff or our hosting provider.

Sales files sometimes contain data about your customers, such as names or phone numbers from delivery orders. As soon as a file is opened, and before any analysis, the app removes columns whose names indicate customer personal data (for example "Customer Name", "Mobile", "Email", "Address", "Customer ID"). This happens on your device. The app never needs this data, and we recommend you remove it from exports before opening them.

Because this processing happens on your device and not by us, we do not act as a Data Fiduciary or Data Processor for the contents of your files. You remain responsible for any personal data you choose to keep in your own files.

7. Data outside India

Some of the providers above process data outside India. Section 16 of the DPDP Act and Rule 15 of the DPDP Rules permit this unless the Central Government restricts transfers to a particular country. We will stop or change any transfer that becomes restricted.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your sales and menu files: never received, so never kept.
  • Visit and technical data: kept by our hosting and delivery providers under their own retention policies for security and operations. We don't keep separate copies.
  • Emails and grievance records: kept for as long as needed to resolve your query, and then for up to one year to show how complaints were handled, unless a longer period is required by law. After that they are deleted.

Under Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act, we erase personal data once its purpose is served and no law requires us to keep it.

9. Security and breaches

The strongest safeguard is architectural: we never receive your business files. In addition, the site is served only over encrypted HTTPS with a strict content security policy, has no login or database that could leak, and loads no advertising or tracking scripts.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay and inform the Data Protection Board of India, as Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act and Rule 7 of the DPDP Rules require. We will report cyber security incidents to CERT-In within the time set by its directions of 28 April 2022.

13. Children

14. Cookies and browser storage

We set no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. To work, the app relies on your browser's normal cache to keep downloaded software ready for next time, and on temporary memory while the tab is open. You can clear both at any time in your browser settings. Your uploaded files are never written to browser storage.
