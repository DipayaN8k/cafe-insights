How it works

No installation, no account, no sales calls.

  1. Export your sales

    Download the item-wise sales report from Petpooja, Vyapar, myBillBook or any billing app, as Excel or CSV. Add your menu with each item's cost to unlock profit.

  2. Open it here

    The app cleans up the export for you: spelling variations, blank rows, duplicates and unfamiliar column names.

  3. Act on the advice

    Every finding comes with a plain next step. If there isn't enough history yet, the app says so instead of guessing.

Questions owners ask

Do you see my sales figures?

No. The analysis runs in your browser using WebAssembly, so your file is never sent to us. We couldn't look at it even if we wanted to.

Which billing apps does it work with?

Any app that can export item-wise sales to Excel or CSV. Common column names like "Bill No", "Item Name", "Qty" and "Rate" are recognised automatically.

I opened my cafe last week. Can I use it?

Not yet, and we'd rather tell you that than guess. Record every sale for four weeks and note what each item costs you. After that the insights unlock.

Why does it ask for item costs?

Revenue alone hides the truth: a best-seller can be your least profitable item. With costs, the menu map ranks items by what they actually earn.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The first load takes around 20 seconds while the analysis engine downloads; after that your browser keeps it ready.
