Cancellation and refund policy

Effective 31 October 2026.

  1. Today: everything is free
  2. Cancelling a subscription
  3. When we refund
  4. One-time reports
  5. How to ask
  6. Timelines

1. Today: everything is free

Cafe Insights is free during early access. Nothing is charged, so there is nothing to cancel or refund. The rest of this policy applies once paid plans launch, and prices will be shown before you pay.

2. Cancelling a subscription

  • You can cancel a monthly or yearly plan at any time from your account or by emailing business.dipayan.sayak@gmail.com. You can also revoke the e-mandate through your bank or UPI app.
  • After cancelling, no further payments are taken. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • We don't charge cancellation fees.

3. When we refund

Because the Service is digital and available as soon as you pay, the period already used is not usually refundable. We will give a full refund if:

  • you were charged twice for the same period, or charged after cancelling;
  • a technical fault on our side stopped the paid features from working and we couldn't fix it within 7 days of your report;
  • you ask within 7 days of your first payment for a plan, as a new customer, for any reason;
  • we end the Service before your paid period is over. In that case we refund the unused portion pro rata.

4. One-time reports

If you buy a one-time report and it can't be produced from a valid sales file, we'll either fix the problem within 7 days or refund you in full.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email business.dipayan.sayak@gmail.com with the payment date, amount, and the transaction or order ID from your receipt. Don't send card numbers, UPI PINs or passwords; we will never ask for them.

6. Timelines

We acknowledge refund requests within 48 hours and decide within 7 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. They normally reach you within 5–7 working days after approval, depending on your bank. Unresolved issues can be raised with our Grievance Officer through the Contact page.
