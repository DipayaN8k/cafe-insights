CAFE INSIGHTS - DEMO PACK
=========================

What's in this folder
---------------------
1_cafe_sales_export.csv   One year of item-level sales (79,782 rows), laid out like a billing-app export:
                          Bill No, Bill Date, Item Name, Qty, Rate, Order Type, Customer Name, Mobile No
2_menu_with_costs.csv     18 menu items with price and cost to make (unlocks profit)
3_past_weather.csv        Daily max temperature and rain for the same year (sharpens the forecast)

IMPORTANT: this is SIMULATED data for a fictional Mumbai cafe, generated by
scripts/generate_sample_data.py with realistic patterns planted on purpose. Say so when you
present it. The customer names and phone numbers are fake placeholders (e.g. "Customer 9503",
"98XXXX9503") included to show the privacy feature.

How to run the demo (about 3 minutes)
-------------------------------------
1. Open the app and choose "Upload my files" in the sidebar.
2. Upload 1_cafe_sales_export.csv as the Sales file.
3. Upload 2_menu_with_costs.csv as the Menu file, and 3_past_weather.csv as Past weather.
4. Leave "Dates are written day first" ticked and currency as ₹.
5. Open "What we cleaned in your file" and point out:
   - customer names and phone numbers were removed automatically (privacy)
   - your POS column names (Bill No, Item Name, Qty, Rate) were matched automatically
   - 328 misspelt item names were fixed (Cappucino -> Cappuccino)
   - 60 duplicate rows removed, 40 blank quantities filled
6. Walk through "Your top actions this week", then each tab.

What the audience should see
----------------------------
- "Just 10 of your 18 items bring in 81% of your money."
- Winners: Cappuccino, Veg Sandwich, Cafe Latte, Cold Coffee, Iced Latte, Paneer Wrap, Chicken Sandwich
- Popular, low profit: Butter Croissant, Masala Chai, Chocolate Muffin (with suggested price rises)
- Hidden gems: Iced Tea, Blueberry Cheesecake, Cold Brew, Hot Chocolate, Mango Smoothie
- Under-performers: Espresso, Banana Bread, Avocado Toast
- Item rule: "Butter Croissant: prep 28% fewer on Mondays"
- Top combo: Iced Latte + Blueberry Cheesecake ("just ask, no discount")
- Forecast accuracy (under "How accurate is this?"): daily totals within about 8%

Why the results match what was planted
---------------------------------------
The generator hid these patterns on purpose: busier weekends, heat boosting cold drinks,
rain boosting hot drinks, fewer croissants on Mondays, and specific pairs bought together.
The app finding exactly these is the proof that the analysis works.

For a demo on REAL data, download the French bakery dataset (link in the app's sidebar,
"Practice with real cafe data"), choose € as the currency, and switch on "Estimate them for me".
