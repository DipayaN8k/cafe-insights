For complaints about the Service, about how personal data is handled, or about a payment, contact our Grievance Officer. This role is required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Rule 5(9) of the SPDI Rules, 2011 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
